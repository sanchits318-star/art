✨ Why Technologies Matter ✨

Technologies introduce children to tools, devices, and digital resources that help them explore, create, and solve problems. By using simple machines, computers, tablets, and coding toys, children develop creativity, critical thinking, and confidence in experimenting with technology. Early exposure to technology encourages children to ask questions, design solutions, and test ideas safely. These experiences help children understand cause and effect, develop planning and problem-solving skills, and express creativity in new ways across all curriculum areas.

🧠 Creativity Theories 🧠

The Reggio Emilia approach (Malaguzzi, 1996) values technology as a medium for children to express ideas and document learning. Vygotsky (1978) emphasised learning through guided interaction and collaborative problem-solving, which aligns with using technology in group activities. Gardner’s theory of multiple intelligences highlights logical-mathematical and visual-spatial skills, supporting creativity in programming, building, and designing with technology. Activities involving technology foster experimentation, reflection, and iterative problem-solving, helping children develop both cognitive and technical skills.

🚀 Super Tech Adventures 🚀

👶 0–2 Years

1. Exploring Cause & Effect with Buttons – press → lights & sounds → magic!
2. Digital Picture Exploration – swipe & talk about animals, colours & stories

🐝 2–3 Years

1. Bee-Bot Floor Paths – program the bee to visit the flowers!
2. Interactive Story Apps – touch the sun → it shines!

🧑‍💻 3–5 Years

1. Coding with ScratchJr – make the cat dance & the dragon fly!
2. Building a Simple Machine – levers, pulleys & ramps to save the day

🤖 6–8 Years

1. Design a Robot Challenge – build & program your own robot friend
2. Digital Storytelling Project – create interactive stories with photos, voice & animation
